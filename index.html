wen gong agent

A personal job-application assistant. Not a public service.

wen gong agent is a private tool used by one person, its owner, to organize their own job applications. It runs on the owner's computer and keeps a list of job postings and draft application materials in the owner's own Google account.

With the owner's permission, it uses two Google services in that one account:

  • Google Sheets, to read and update the owner's job-application tracking sheet.
  • Google Drive, limited to files the app itself creates (draft resumes and cover letters).

It does not offer sign-up, is not available to other users, and does not submit job applications.
