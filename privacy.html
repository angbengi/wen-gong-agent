Privacy policy

wen gong agent · Last updated October 14, 2026

wen gong agent is a personal tool used only by its owner. This policy explains what Google data it uses and why.

Who uses it

Only the owner, signed in with their own Google account. The app has no other users and no sign-up.

What Google data it accesses

  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets scope): to read and update the owner's job-application tracking sheet.
  • Google Drive (drive.file scope): only files the app itself creates, such as draft resumes and cover letters. It cannot see other files in the account.

How the data is used

The data is used only to help the owner track and prepare their own job applications. It stays in the owner's Google account and on the owner's computer.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising. Text from job postings may be processed by an AI model provider solely to draft the owner's application materials. Google user data is not used to train AI models.

Storage and deletion

The app's sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the sheet and files from their own Google Drive.

Google API Services User Data Policy

wen gong agent's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
